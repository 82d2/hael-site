how to use hæl

by the maker of hæl · november 2, 2026

the short answer

turn the dial to the hours you want and press begin. the ring keeps time, and the words beneath it change with each stretch of the fast. change the goal or the start by tapping it; end the fast when you choose, and the ledger keeps it. the timer, the ledger and rest days are free.

hæl is a fasting timer for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. the timer, the ledger and rest days are free.

1. set the hours

home opens on the dial. turn it to choose how long the fast will be; the number in the middle is the goal, in hours. turn past the top and it counts in days.

2. begin

press begin. the ring fills as the hours pass, and beneath it are the time and the stretch of the fast you are in: the threshold, at hour six. each stretch has its own writing, so the words under the ring change as the fast goes on.

3. change the goal, or when it began

under the ring are the goal and the time the fast will end. tap them to change either. if you forgot to press begin, move the start back to when you really stopped eating. it can go back up to seven days, but never into the fast before.

4. long fasts

set a goal of forty-eight hours or more and hæl first shows a page saying that fasts this long should be medically supervised. it says so once, plainly, and leaves the choice with you.

5. end the fast

press end fast whenever you are ready, at the goal or before it. the fast goes into the ledger as it was kept.

6. rest days

under begin is rest. press it on a day you won't fast, and the ledger keeps the day as rest rather than a gap.

7. the ledger

the ledger, at the foot of home, keeps your fasts and rest days week by week.

8. notifications

if you allow them, hæl sends a quiet line when a fast begins, at some of its milestones, and when it reaches its goal. there are no streaks to keep and no reminders to do better.

9. the watch and the home screen

the watch shows the fast in progress, and widgets keep it on the home and lock screens. hæl also runs on iPad.

the full witness

the timer, the ledger, the daily greeting and rest days are free and complete. the full witness adds the cycle: the ring in the colour of the phase, hours suggested for each phase, weight read against the cycle, every fast back to the first, and a letter each monday about the week you kept. it opens for fourteen days, free, once, and nothing renews. how hæl reads the cycle.

hæl is on the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, with no account to make.

hæl observes; it does not diagnose or treat anything, and nothing here is medical advice. if something about your body worries you, see a doctor.

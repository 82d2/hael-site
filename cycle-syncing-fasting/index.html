cycle syncing your fasts, in hæl

by the maker of hæl · november 2, 2026

the short answer

tell hæl when your last period began, or connect Apple Health, and it knows the phase you are in. it suggests a range of hours for each phase, shorter around the period and before it, longer after it, and reads your weight against the same point of the cycle. it is part of the full witness, and every suggestion stays a suggestion.

hæl, a fasting timer for iPhone, reads the phase from Apple Health or your last period and suggests the hours for it.

set it up

when you first open hæl, it asks when your last period began, or whether a cycle applies to you at all. you can also connect Apple Health: with your permission, hæl reads and writes period starts and weight there, so a period logged in another app reaches hæl too.

the hours for each phase

hæl suggests a range of hours for the phase you are in:

  • menstrual: twelve to fourteen hours
  • follicular: sixteen to eighteen hours
  • ovulation: sixteen to twenty hours
  • luteal: fourteen to sixteen hours

the ranges follow how a fast tends to feel in each phase: shorter while bleeding and in the days before a period, longer in the stretch after it. no trial has tested fasting by phase, so hæl keeps them a suggestion and never a rule; the dial still turns to any length. what the research does and doesn't show is in intermittent fasting and the menstrual cycle.

the ring takes the colour of the phase

with the full witness open, the ring on home wears the colour of the phase you are in, and the writing during a fast follows the phase too.

weight, read against the cycle

log a morning weight in form, or let it come from Apple Health. the body ledger learns where your weight usually sits on each day of the cycle and reads each morning against that, with the same point of last cycle beside it, never against yesterday. why weight goes up before a period explains what it is reading.

without a cycle

on hormonal birth control, after menopause, or whenever a cycle doesn't apply, turn cycle tracking off in settings. the ring keeps the hours alone, and the body ledger compares each morning with the same time last month.

where it is kept

your cycle and your weight stay on your phone, in your own iCloud, and in Apple Health if you connect it. there is no account and no server of ours.

what is free

the timer, the ledger and rest days are free. fasting by the cycle is part of the full witness, which opens for fourteen days, free, once, and nothing renews. how to use hæl, step by step.

hæl is on the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

hæl observes; it does not diagnose or treat anything, and nothing here is medical advice. if something about your body worries you, see a doctor.

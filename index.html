intermittent fasting, shaped by the cycle

most fasting apps count down and celebrate. hæl counts and stays quiet. the interface is a single ring. the language is literary. the body's rhythm shapes what the app says and when.

an ensō ring marks the hours. the timer carries a name that changes as the fast deepens. lauds, prime, terce, vespers. names borrowed from the canonical hours. prose arrives about what the body is doing, not what it should do.

hæl reads the menstrual cycle through Apple Health. the ring tint shifts with the phase. fasting windows adjust. shorter during menstruation, longer during follicular. weight is read in cycle context. a gain during luteal is not a gain during ovulation. the app knows the difference.

weekly letters arrive on mondays. a retrospective of the week. the fasting, the weight, the cycle. monthly readings take the longer view. these are not reports. they are readings.

when a fast ends, a page stays. the date spelled out, the hours witnessed, a line composed from what happened. a small ceremony for something the body did.

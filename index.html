does hæl work without a period, or on birth control?

yes. cycle tracking can be turned off in settings, and then the ring keeps the hours alone and the body ledger compares each morning with this time last month instead of with the cycle.

is it a subscription?

no. the fasting timer is free and complete. the full witness, which reads the cycle and the body over time, is one purchase of $19.99 in the US, with nothing recurring.

does it work with Apple Health?

yes, with your permission. hæl reads and writes period starts and weight, and can record each fast as a mind and body session if you turn that on. it reads resting heart rate for the weekly letter.

where does my data go?

to your phone, your own iCloud, and Apple Health. there is no account and no server of ours. anonymous usage counts go to TelemetryDeck and purchases are checked through RevenueCat; neither sees your records.

does it run on Apple Watch and iPad?

yes. the watch shows the fast in progress, widgets keep it on the home and lock screens, and the app runs on iPad.

is hæl medical advice?

no. it observes; it does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. fasts beyond forty-eight hours should be medically supervised, and hæl says so each time. settings links to eating disorder support.

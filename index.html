i–ii. the hours and the page

a new page
for every hour.

begin a fast and the ring keeps time. each stretch of it has a name, from lauds to vigil, and the page beneath the ring is written for the hour you are in. no streak flames, no badges, no voice telling you to push.

iii. the water

the water,
given room.

weight rises before a period, and most apps read it as failure. hæl learns where your body carries water across the cycle and reads each morning against that, never against yesterday. last cycle stays beside it, faintly, for comparison.

iv. the cycle

the cycle, read.

connect Apple Health or enter your last period, and the ring takes the color of the phase. the phase suggests the hours, and the words follow it. if a cycle doesn't apply to you, turn it off and the ring keeps the hours alone.

v. the letters

a letter
on monday.

the full witness writes to you each monday about the week you kept: the fasts, the rest, the weight read against the cycle. it notices; it never grades.

each month, a reading. each season, another. and at the year’s end, the year.

vii. questions

questions.

is there a free trial?

yes. the full witness is free for fourteen days, once. nothing is charged and nothing renews; when the days end it closes on its own, and everything you kept stays.

does hæl work without a period, or on birth control?

yes. cycle tracking can be turned off in settings, and then the ring keeps the hours alone and the body ledger compares each morning with this time last month instead of with the cycle.

is it a subscription?

no. the fasting timer is free and complete. the full witness, which reads the cycle and the body over time, is free for fourteen days, then one purchase of $19.99 in the US, with nothing recurring.

does it work with Apple Health?

yes, with your permission. hæl reads and writes period starts and weight, and can record each fast as a mind and body session if you turn that on. it reads resting heart rate for the weekly letter.

where does my data go?

to your phone, your own iCloud, and Apple Health. there is no account and no server of ours. anonymous usage counts go to TelemetryDeck and purchases are checked through RevenueCat; neither sees your records.

does it run on Apple Watch and iPad?

yes. the watch shows the fast in progress, widgets keep it on the home and lock screens, and the app runs on iPad.

is hæl medical advice?

no. it observes; it does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. fasts beyond forty-eight hours should be medically supervised, and hæl says so each time. settings links to eating disorder support.

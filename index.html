intermittent fasting, shaped by the cycle

most fasting apps count down and congratulate. hæl counts and stays quiet. the interface is a single ring. the language is literary. the body's rhythm shapes what the app says and when.

hæl reads the menstrual cycle through Apple Health. fasting suggestions adjust with the phase: shorter windows during menstruation, longer during follicular. weight readings are interpreted in cycle context. a gain during luteal is not the same as a gain during ovulation. the app knows the difference.

weekly letters arrive on mondays. a retrospective of the week's fasting, written in a quiet editorial voice. monthly readings take the longer view. these are not reports. they are readings.
